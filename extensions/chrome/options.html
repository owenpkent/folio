Folio PDF Viewer

When you open a PDF

Replaces Chrome's built-in PDF reader. You can still hand the document to the desktop app at any time.

Chrome's reader stays in charge. The toolbar button and right-click menu still open the current PDF in the Folio desktop app.

Folio does nothing until you turn it back on.

Sites to leave alone

PDFs on these sites open in Chrome's reader as usual. Subdomains are included, so example.com also covers docs.example.com.
